About us

We build the way Wayanad lives.

N Arch Builders & Designers is a construction and design company based in Sulthan Bathery. We take on homes, interiors, renovations and landscapes, and we look after them from the first sketch to the handover.

Our story

One team from drawing board to doorstep.

N Arch began with a simple idea: the person who designs your house should be responsible for building it. That keeps drawings honest, costs clear and finishes true to what you approved.

We work close to home. Our office sits opposite Victory Hospital in Sulthan Bathery, so site visits, material sourcing and follow-up are always a short drive away.

Mission and approach

What guides every project.

Mission

Honest building

To deliver well-built, well-designed spaces that families in Wayanad are proud to live in, at a cost agreed in advance.

Approach

Plan the land first

We study slope, drainage, sun and rain before choosing a layout, so the house suits the plot and the season.

Promise

No surprises

Clear drawings, a written estimate and regular site updates, so you always know where your project stands.
